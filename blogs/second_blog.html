Chapter 2 : From A Humble Jewish Girl to The Mother of Christ and Church

Whom do you think spent the most time with Jesus? Was it the Disciples, St. Joseph, or the Pharisees? No, it was Mother Mary who was at the birth, death, resurrection and descent of the Holy Spirit. The first person who played an important role in the early church is Mother Mary. It all started with a simple "Yes" showing a humble surrenderance.

While there were multiple things going around like the visitation of Gabriel during the incarnaion, Simeon prophesying during the presentation at the temple and the 12 year old Jesus staying behind in Jerusalem. Mother Mary just treasured all these words and pondered them in her heart [Luke 2:19]. That shows her trust and faith in the Lord.

Even during the wedding at Cana, while the disciples drunk dry all the wine (just kidding), Mother Mary made sure that the house would not be put to shame. She trusted in Christ, even when he had not performed any miracles earlier. That must have taken a giant leap of Faith.

Most importantly, the part where he calls her "Woman" refers directly to the promise of God in Genesis 3:15, making her the "New Eve". And the Marian Dogmas of Immaculate Conception explaining how God showed her a singular grace through the merits of his life death and resurrection from her conception to be saved from the stain sin and of the Original Sin. Further Dogmas of Theotokos - Mother of God, Virginitas Perpetua - Perpetual Virginity, Assumptio Baetae - Assumption into heaven, shows us how God has raised his humble servant into unimaginable glory. These profound truths reveal why generations have held her in the highest veneration and to understand the importance of her intercessions.
